Why this page exists

Same buddy-core.js, same face geometry, same state machine — a different physics underneath. An e-paper pixel is bistable: once set, it stays set with the power removed. An OLED must be driven continuously or it goes dark. That single fact changes the whole product: the pet becomes something that changes occasionally rather than animates.

The refresh model, simulated here

OperationCostWhy
Hold an image0 mJphysics, not electronics
Partial refresh~0.4 s, ~15 mArewrites changed pixels; leaves faint ghosting
Full refresh~2.4 s, ~20 mAinverts the whole panel to clear ghosting
Deep sleep~9 µAMCU only; the panel draws nothing

What carries over from the OLED build

The framebuffer, the face, the state machine: all of it. What changes is the driver (Adafruit_SSD1306 → GxEPD2), the bus (I²C → SPI, two wires become five), the geometry constants (the face is drawn at 128×64 and blitted at 2× to fill 296×128), and the loop — event-driven instead of delay(60) per frame.

Honest caveat

"No power" is an overstatement. E-paper needs nothing to hold an image, but refreshing costs 15–20 mA for a second or more, the driver idles in the tens of microamps, and the MCU still wakes on a timer. The claim is near-zero standby, not zero.
